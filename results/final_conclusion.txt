- Best Hybrid alpha = 0.8 (embedding weight) with F1 = 0.974

### Trade-offs between retrieval quality, computational cost, context usage, and accuracy

- **Embedding-only**: Low computational cost at inference, fast vector search. Good for quick filtering. Misses lexical cues and sometimes structural refactors.
- **Direct LLM**: High context usage and API cost; strong reasoning when the corpus fits the model context; not scalable for large corpora.
- **Standard RAG**: Middle ground — good accuracy with moderate cost. Retrieval determines what LLM sees, so retrieval quality is critical.
- **Hybrid RAG**: Best accuracy in most cases due to combined lexical + semantic retrieval; higher implementation complexity and slightly higher compute at retrieval time (BM25 + embeddings).

### Recommendations
- Use embedding-only as a **fast pre-filter**.
- Use RAG for day-to-day plagiarism checks (k=3–5 based on experiments).
- Use Hybrid RAG when high accuracy is required (e.g., grading, legal review), and tune alpha (e.g., 0.4–0.6).
- Use Direct LLM only for small corpora or when you want to do deep reasoning on ambiguous cases.
